✦ Open comment platform

Comments that
belong to you

Commex is an embeddable comment and reaction platform where you own the data. Connect your own MongoDB database and embed stunning comment boxes on any website.

your-website.com/blog/post
<!-- Commex — 2 lines to add comments -->
<div id="commex-widget"></div>
<script>
  window.CommexConfig = {
    orgSlug: "my-org",
    pageUrl: window.location.href
  };
</script>
<script src="commex.app/embed/commex.js" async></script>

Everything you need

🗄️
Your database, your data
Each organization connects to its own MongoDB instance. Your comments never mix with others and you control retention.
⚡
Two-line embed
Add a <div> and a <script> tag. Commex does the rest — auto-detects your page, loads comments instantly.
🎭
Rich reactions
10 built-in emoji reactions plus custom org-specific ones. Upvoting, downvoting, and per-page reactions too.
🧵
Threaded replies
Full nested comment threads with @mentions, notifications, and smart depth limiting to keep conversations readable.
🎞️
GIFs & media
Integrated Tenor GIF search, image uploads, and rich text with markdown formatting built right into the comment box.
🛡️
Moderation tools
Pin comments, flag spam, ban words, approve-before-show mode, and full moderation controls for org admins.
🎨
Fully customizable
Custom accent colors, per-org branding, feature toggles, and domain allowlisting to match your site perfectly.
📱
Responsive & dark
The embed widget is pixel-perfect on mobile, tablet, and desktop. Dark-first design that looks great everywhere.
